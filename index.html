How we work

A disciplined four-stage engagement

Every mandate starts with the same question: what decision are you trying to make? The process is built to answer it and to leave your team stronger afterwards.

  1. 01

    Discovery & diagnostic

    A two-week review of your balance sheet, obligations and growth plans, delivered as a written diagnostic.

  2. 02

    Strategy & structure

    Scenario modelling and measurable objectives agreed with your board before any recommendation is made.

  3. 03

    Implementation

    We execute alongside your finance team — lenders, custodians, counsel and reporting included.

  4. 04

    Review & reporting

    Quarterly reviews with clear attribution, risk metrics and adjustments as markets move.

About Meridian

Independent by design, accountable by contract

Meridian Capital was founded in 1998 by three advisors who wanted to work without product quotas. The firm remains employee-owned, which means the advice we give is the advice we would take ourselves.

Our teams sit across corporate finance, treasury, risk and investment management, and work as one group on every mandate. We take on a limited number of engagements each year so that senior advisors remain directly involved in the work.
